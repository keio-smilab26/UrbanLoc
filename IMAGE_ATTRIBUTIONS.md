## Mapillary images

The street-level images are from [Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com/) and are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each image is credited below in the format that Mapillary gives as an example of a perfect attribution ([Mapillary Help Center](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409)):

> "Title" <link to Mapillary image> by "username" <link to user profile>, licensed under CC BY-SA

Mapillary images have no titles, so each title gives the city and the capture coordinates. All images were resized and cropped to fit the figure layout; no other changes were made.
